## 1. Self-onboarding MVP for a B2B SaaS (Frota 162)

**Role:** Head of Marketing, Growth & Product · 05/2022 to 08/2024 · B2B logistics SaaS

**Context.** Early-stage SaaS, around R$20k MRR and a team of 4 when I joined. Sales cycles could take up to six months, and activating a new customer depended on assisted onboarding by the team.

**Problem.** Assisted onboarding was a bottleneck for both sides of the business: it slowed down the time to value for customers and kept the team busy with repetitive setup work instead of growth.

**What I did.**
- Built a self-onboarding MVP in **Bubble.io**, so we could test the hypothesis without waiting for the engineering roadmap.
- Unified the customer data into a single base connecting **Google, RD Station, ExactSales and Pipedrive** through **Make and Zapier**, so marketing, sales and CS read the same funnel.
- Rebuilt inbound as the main acquisition engine and managed a team of 7 across marketing and CS.

**Results.**
- **Onboarding 80% faster** with the self-onboarding MVP
- **Sales cycle from up to 6 months to 60 days**
- MRR from **~R$20k to ~R$400k**, company from **4 to 40 people**
- **70% of MRR from inbound**, inbound leads from 1 to 6 per month to 60 to 152
- Inbound converted at **7.5% vs 5.2%** for outbound · **ROAS 12** · **churn below 5%**

**Learning.** A no-code MVP is a product decision, not a shortcut. It let us validate the onboarding flow with real customers first, and only then ask engineering for scarce time.

## 2. Attribution and CRM automation at scale (GGPoker)

**Role:** Head of Marketing & Growth · 02/2025 to 04/2026 · team of 15, including 5 managers

**Problem.** Paid media was scaling, but most signups had no tracked origin, which made budget decisions close to guesswork. In CRM, every campaign was built from scratch, and the team was about to hire one more person to keep up.

**What I did.**
- Treated attribution as a product and made source tracking a priority across channels.
- Replaced one-off CRM campaigns with automated flows and reusable processes.
- Used AI for campaign insights, copy and design, and n8n plus AI for competitor monitoring.

**Results.**
- **Signups with no tracked origin from 70% to 16%**
- **+99% signups in 12 months** (8,553 to 17,036)
- Meta Ads scaled from **US$32k to US$129k per month** (about US$881k in 12 months), with **CAC per depositor 33% lower in the first six months**
- Referral program with **CPA around US$5**
- Automated CRM **removed the need for a new hire**

**Learning.** Automation doesn't create results by itself. It frees people to work on the decisions that do.

## 3. AI job-matching system with a human feedback loop (personal build)

**Status:** MVP 1, built and running on self-hosted n8n

**Problem.** Job search is a high-volume, low-signal funnel. Reading every posting manually doesn't scale, and generic keyword filters miss context.

**What I built.**
- **Discovery:** collects job postings automatically (LinkedIn guest jobs API).
- **Scoring:** an LLM (via OpenRouter) scores each posting's fit against a structured profile.
- **Classification and alerts:** results are stored in Google Sheets and pushed to Telegram.
- **Learning loop:** when I override a rejected job, I add a justification. The system turns it into a general fit rule, so the next scoring round gets better.

**Product decisions.**
- Human in the loop on purpose: the model suggests, the human decides, and every disagreement becomes training material for the rules.
- Sheets and Telegram instead of a custom UI, so the MVP ran with zero front-end work.

**Stack:** n8n · OpenRouter · Google Sheets · Telegram · webhooks
